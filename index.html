About Daz Paints

Daz Paints Coatings & Contracting Services (DPCS) is a dedicated supplier of premium paints and coatings for residential, commercial, industrial and marine projects, backed by long-standing relationships with our clients and suppliers.

We provide a "One Stop Solution" for choosing the right paint. DPCS has the expertise to meet customer requirements with top-of-the-line products at affordable prices, without compromising on quality.

Operational in Oman since 2023, we specialize in project-to-project supplies and prefer challenging items where consultants face sourcing issues or budget constraints.
